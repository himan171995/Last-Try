Method

How these times were made

drikpanchang.com can't be reached from the environment that built this report, so these times are calculated rather than copied from the site. The calculation uses the same steps the site does:

  1. Sunrise and sunset for New Delhi (28.61° N, 77.21° E): the moment the Sun's upper edge crosses the horizon, allowing for standard refraction.
  2. Day and night each split into five equal yamas.
  3. The order of activities, ratings and relations for each bird, weekday, fortnight and half-day, from the data table in the open-source panchapakshi-engine (MIT). That table was built from Drik Panchang pages.
  4. Each sub-period's share of 144 parts stretched to the real length of its yama, then rounded to the nearest minute.

Today is the fourth day of the waxing fortnight. The new moon ended on 10 October and the full moon is on 26 October, so the fortnight doesn't change during the day.

Pancha Pakshi Shastra comes from the Tamil Siddha tradition. Your bird is set by your birth nakshatra and fortnight. Each yama, your bird does one main activity. Each yama is split into five sub-periods, one per bird. Ruling and Eating are the strong states, Sleeping and Dying the weak ones. The strength values come from VedAstro and the IJMRME paper.

This is a traditional timing system and not a forecast. Sites that publish it warn against treating it as a guarantee of financial, legal or medical outcomes.
